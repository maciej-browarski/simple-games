Pick 6 from 49

See how many numbers you can match today.

How to Play:

  1. Fill your coupon: Pick exactly 6 numbers from the 1 to 49 grid.
  2. Start the draw: Click the PLAY button. The lottery numbers will start flashing rapidly.
  3. Lock the result: Press the STOP button at any moment to freeze the winning numbers.
  4. See your score: The game automatically checks your coupon. Your final result will appear in the Numbers matched: [X] section.
